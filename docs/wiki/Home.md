# ArtifactFlow

**Your AI work, in one place.**

ArtifactFlow is an open-source, self-hosted workspace for AI-generated artifacts.
Keep useful outputs from different tools, find them with your team, and continue
working through authorized MCP-compatible clients.

It preserves deliberate outputs, not conversations. There is no automatic
chat capture, built-in AI generation, or required model subscription.
